Embroidery vs. screen print

Office pieces are always embroidered in full colour. Hi-vis field pieces are screen printed — you choose 1-colour (cost-effective, high contrast) or 2-colour (closer to your full logo) for the whole program.

Always readable, on every garment

Ink and thread are matched to contrast with the garment at the exact placement — dark ink on hi-vis and light garments, white reverse on navy and black — so the mark never gets lost.
